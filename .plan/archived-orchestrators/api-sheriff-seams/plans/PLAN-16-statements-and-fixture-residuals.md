## Objective

PLAN-15 shipped the cause mapping, the fixture's release on every path, the renames, the veto helper and the class-loader fix, and its analysis left what is this repository's own: three statements that still say the request timeout alone ends a stalled body on JDK 27 while the probe the plan itself changed says it may end with the JDK's own failure, a Javadoc sentence that says the `ParserConfig` is pre-initialized on the caller's thread when its `DslJson` is built lazily, fixtures and tests that leak or assert nothing on a failure path, a wiring test that pins a helper and not the call, a probe that accepts any `IOException`, framed names that are left, a broad catch the project rules do not list, and two reads that were never run.
